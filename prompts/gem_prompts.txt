Pile composition and scale — applies to every variant
Use equal-sized gems, small varied rotations, and partial overlaps. Keep exactly the requested count individually recognizable from exposed faces and rims. Favor a broad, bottom-heavy mound, slightly wider than tall, with foreground gems covering the lower edges of rear gems. Never stretch gems or shrink rear gems with perspective. No isolated outliers or hidden extra gems.
The visible pile must fit within THREE single-gem widths and TWO single-gem heights, including rotated corners. These are maximum bounds, not a target to fill for small counts. Increase overlap to fit; do not reduce gem size. Leave only a small transparent margin outside these bounds.
Row descriptions below run BACK TO FRONT. For two rows, overlap vertically by about half a gem height. For three rows, separate row centers by at most 0.45 gem heights. Within rows, overlap as needed; three-across rows should span about 2.4–2.8 gem widths. Minor staggering and rotations must stay within the overall bounds.
