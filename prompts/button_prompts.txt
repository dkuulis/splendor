Rectangular enamel-and-gold buttons — approved palette.
Generated with the built-in image tool, one call per source artwork variant.
The approved maroon sample is reused as the Take source.

Layout: 140 x 42 px per button. Vertical order: Buy, Reserve, Take, Restart.
Top positions: 5, 55, 105, 163 px. Gaps: 8, 8, 16 px.
Total: 5 + 4*42 + 8 + 8 + 16 + 5 = 210 px.
All sources and runtime assets are fully opaque rectangles with square corners.
Enabled artwork is action-specific; disabled artwork is shared.
Labels use font/Forum-Regular.ttf at 20 px, rendered at 8x and downsampled.
Enabled labels: cream #FFF0CC. Disabled labels: muted cream #C9C2B4.

Labels: Buy, Reserve, Take, Restart. Take keeps its label with zero selected
gems: clicking it ends the turn without collecting gems and counts toward
the existing stalemate rule. Nonempty selections keep the existing limits.
Restart remains available during AI turns and after the game ends.

Palette: Buy sapphire blue #245A86; Reserve emerald green #246747;
Take maroon #702C3C; Restart charcoal bronze #34332C.
Disabled: matte gray-brown #504B42 with a dull bronze rim.

Original generated PNGs: images/button_*.png (ignored by Git).
Source artwork: artwork/button_*.webp (quality 80, fully opaque).
Runtime assets: assets/button_<action>_<enabled|disabled>.png (140x42 RGB).
Regenerate runtime assets and an actual-size enabled/disabled preview:
  .venv/bin/python tools/generate_buttons.py --preview images/buttons_preview.png

Exact image-generation prompts follow. The Take prompt originally requested
an approval sample; that approved original is now used in production.
