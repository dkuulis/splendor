Shared prompt
Create a full-bleed square noble portrait for a Renaissance trade game, readable at 150×150px. Depict {high lord or high lady} in the {gem} environment, following the individual brief below. Use realistic painterly artwork, rich natural materials, atmospheric depth, and broad, clearly defined forms. Match the development-card illustrations in prompts/card_prompts.txt, the antique-gold arabesque ornament, and the polished gemstones in prompts/gem_prompts.txt. Use restrained decorative detail and a thin muted border. Show exactly one dignified adult noble in a waist-up or chest-up portrait, with a distinctive face, silhouette, and elegant ceremonial clothing. Keep the face in the upper center-right and leave quiet areas at the top-left and lower-left for later game overlays. Make the noble the main subject, with one recognizable environmental landmark behind them. Flat front view of the tile; no surrounding tabletop, cast shadow outside the tile, title, lettering, numbers, cost badges, point symbols, game icons, logos, or watermark. Avoid tiny details and visual clutter.

Set composition
Create exactly ten fictional nobles: one High Lord and one High Lady from each of the five gem environments. Replace the historical people entirely; do not copy their likenesses or render their names. Vary age, facial features, hairstyle, posture, and clothing between portraits while keeping the shared painting style and layout. Lords and ladies have equal visual authority. Use gem-colored jewelry as part of their clothing, not as a game bonus icon. Each portrait's gem identifies its scenery only; it does not grant a permanent gem bonus or determine its requirements.

Gem environments
Diamond — High mountains. Sharp snow-covered peaks, glaciers, alpine palaces, and crisp daylight. Ivory, silver, and pale-blue tones; clear white, octagonal diamond jewelry.
Sapphire — Sea. Deep blue water, merchant ships, sea cliffs, and distant harbors under bright skies. Blue silks and maritime court dress; vivid blue, heptagonal sapphire jewelry.
Emerald — Forest. Colossal ancient trees, emerald foliage, moss-covered ruins, and sunbeams through the canopy. Architecture integrated into woodland; bright green, rounded-square cushion-cut emerald jewelry.
Ruby — Fire. Volcanic citadels, lava rivers, glowing furnaces, and dark stone. Red court robes and warm metal accents, with readable skin tones against orange-red light; luminous red, round ruby jewelry.
Onyx — Dark cave. Obsidian halls, glowing crystal veins, underground lakes, and warm torchlight against blue-black shadows. Dark velvets and restrained gold; near-black cushion-cut onyx jewelry with bright reflective facets. Keep faces and clothing silhouettes readable.

Files and production stages
images/noble_<gem>_<number>.png — High-resolution square original, preferably at least 1200×1200px; artwork only, with no points or requirements. The images/ directory is ignored by Git.
artwork/noble_<gem>_<number>.webp — Tracked quality-80 WebP source converted from the matching original; retain its square aspect ratio and high resolution. Opaque RGB is appropriate for these full-bleed portraits. No points or requirements.
assets/noble_<gem>_<number>.png — Final 150×150px runtime tile, composed from the matching artwork with the exact points and requirements below. Preserve the same filename stem through all three stages.
Optional high-resolution composed previews belong in images/noble_previews/, using the same filename stem; never replace clean originals with previews.

Asset layout and finishing
All coordinates and sizes below refer to the final 150×150px tile. Compose at 8× resolution (1200×1200px), scaling every position, font, stroke, border, square, and gap by the same factor; downsample once using LANCZOS. Preserve aspect ratio and do not stretch the portraits.

Top-left: Always display the victory-point numeral 3. Match tools/generate_cards.py: bundled assets/Forum-Regular.ttf at 48px with synthetic bold, visible top-left anchor at (6, 12), white fill, dark #25394e outline. At 8× resolution draw the numeral first with a 9px dark stroke, then with a 4px white stroke. These stroke widths are high-resolution pixels, matching the card generator. No point label, badge, or extra symbol.

Top-right: Artwork only; nobles have no bonus-gem icon.

Lower-left: Show the required counts of owned development-card bonuses in a bottom-aligned vertical column of COLORED SQUARES, not circles or gemstone tokens. These are bonus-card requirements, not a token payment. Use each noble's exact requirement metadata above. Order nonzero requirements from top to bottom as Diamond, Sapphire, Emerald, Ruby, Onyx, matching game/constants.py TOKENS; omit zero requirements and gem names. Each square is 26×26px with a 1px muted-gold #c5b58d outline, left edge x=6, and a 4px vertical gap. The lowest square starts at y=120 and ends at y=146; preceding squares start at y=90 and y=60 as needed. Two-requirement nobles use y=90 and y=120; three-requirement nobles use y=60, y=90, and y=120.

Square fills: Diamond #e5e7eb; Sapphire #2563eb; Emerald #15803d; Ruby #be123c; Onyx #334155. Center each requirement numeral at (19, square_y + 13), using Forum at 16px with the same synthetic-bold treatment as card costs (2px text stroke at 8× resolution). Use #111827 text on Diamond and white on every other color. No separate gem icon or outer requirement panel.

Keep the portrait's face, jewelry focal point, and recognizable scenery clear of both overlay areas. Produce bright enabled assets; disabled treatment belongs to the UI.
