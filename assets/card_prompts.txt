Shared prompt
Create a full-bleed 5:7 portrait development-card illustration for a Renaissance trade game, readable at 150×210px. Depict {scene} in the {gem} environment at tier {level}. Use realistic painterly artwork, rich natural materials, atmospheric depth, and broad, clearly defined forms. Flat front view of the card; no surrounding scene, cast shadow, title, lettering, numbers, logos, or watermark. Avoid tiny details and visual clutter.

Diamond — High mountains. Sharp snow-covered peaks, glaciers, mountain passes, alpine fortresses, and bright crisp light. Prestige, purity, and nobility, with ivory, silver, and pale-blue tones.
